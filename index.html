Holly Hill

grown, gathered & occasionally auctioned

Four small businesses, one hillside outside New Lexington: mushrooms we grow, honey we keep, eggs we gather, and the occasional government-surplus find we can't pass up.

A note from the hill

We started with a few grow bags in a spare room and a couple of hens. It grew from there — a honeybee hive, a proper mushroom setup, and along the way, a habit of finding good things at government surplus auctions and passing them on. Everything here is small-batch, seasonal, and looked after by hand.

— The Isham Family
